Privacy Policy

Bible First: Verse Lock · Last updated November 13, 2026

Bible First ("the app") is built so that your Bible reading and your app-locking choices stay on your own device. This policy explains, plainly, what the app does and does not do with your information.

What stays on your device

The app stores the following only in local storage on your phone. It is never sent to us or to anyone else:

  • Your place in the Bible, liked and saved verses
  • Your daily reading goal, streak, and reading history
  • Which apps you've chosen to lock, and your lock settings
  • Whether you've completed onboarding

If you delete the app, this information is deleted with it. We have no way to see, back up, or recover it.

App Screen Time lock

On iPhone, Bible First uses Apple's Screen Time (Family Controls) framework to lock apps you choose until you've read. Apple's framework only tells the app how many apps and categories you picked — the app never learns which specific apps you chose, and never sees how you use other apps. On Android, the lock works through an Accessibility Service that runs entirely on your device; it is used only to detect when a locked app opens, never to read your screen content or monitor other activity.

This feature restricts only the device it's installed on. Bible First does not monitor, track, or report on anyone else's device or usage.

Purchases and subscriptions

Bible First Premium purchases are handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Play Store) and processed through RevenueCat, a subscription infrastructure provider. RevenueCat receives your purchase and subscription status so the app can confirm you have Premium — it does not receive your name, payment details, or Bible reading activity. See RevenueCat's privacy policy for details on how they handle this data.

AI Bible study (Premium)

When you use a study tool (like "Explain simply" or "Context") on a verse, the Bible reference and translation you're studying are sent to our server so we can generate and cache that explanation. We do not send your identity, reading history, or any other personal information along with that request.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, and no ad-tracking SDKs of any kind
  • No selling or sharing of your data with data brokers
  • No account or sign-up is required to use the app
  • No analytics that identify you personally

Children's privacy

Bible First is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app's core features (reading, the lock, streaks) work without any account or personal information at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
