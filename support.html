Managing or cancelling your subscription

Subscriptions are billed by Apple or Google, not by us, so refunds and cancellations go through them directly:

iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Bible First.
Android: Google Play app → Menu → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.

App Lock not working?

Make sure Screen Time (iPhone) or Accessibility access (Android) is turned on for Bible First in the Lock tab, and that you've selected at least one app to lock. If a shield or lock screen doesn't appear when you expect it to, try closing and reopening the locked app once.
